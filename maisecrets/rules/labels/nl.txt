# Dutch credential labels.
# Fields, tab-separated: a lowercase literal every match contains, the regex, examples.
# (?<![^\W\d_]) is the start of a word: no letter of any script before it, while _ and a digit may
# stand there (db_...). The sources of every word: docs/label-sources.md.
# wachtwoord: no word start, because Dutch joins compounds (beheerderswachtwoord).
wachtwoord	wachtwoord	wachtwoord, Wachtwoord, db_wachtwoord, nieuw wachtwoord, beheerderswachtwoord
# sleutel alone is an ordinary word (key): only the two-word labels and the compound count.
sleutel	(?<![^\W\d_])(?:geheime[ _-]|api[ _-]?)sleutel	geheime sleutel, Geheime sleutel, API-sleutel
inloggegevens	(?<![^\W\d_])inloggegevens	inloggegevens, Inloggegevens
