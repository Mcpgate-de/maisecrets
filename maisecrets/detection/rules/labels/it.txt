# Italian credential labels. Italian interfaces write password, which the denylist has already.
# Fields, tab-separated: a lowercase literal every match contains, the regex, examples.
# (?<![^\W\d_]) is the start of a word: no letter of any script before it, while _ and a digit may
# stand there (db_...). The sources of every word: docs/label-sources.md.
# chiave alone is an ordinary word (parola chiave: keyword) and segreto alone is too: only the
# labels of two or more words count.
chiave	(?<![^\W\d_])chiave[ _-](?:di[ _-]accesso|segreta|api)	chiave di accesso, Chiave segreta, chiave API, chiave_segreta
segreto	(?<![^\W\d_])segreto[ _-]del[ _-]client	segreto del client, Segreto del client
credenziali	(?<![^\W\d_])credenziali	credenziali, Credenziali, db_credenziali
