# Spanish credential labels. contraseña and contrasena are in the detect-secrets denylist already.
# Fields, tab-separated: a lowercase literal every match contains, the regex, examples.
# (?<![^\W\d_]) is the start of a word: no letter of any script before it, while _ and a digit may
# stand there (db_...). The sources of every word: docs/label-sources.md.
# clave alone is an ordinary word (palabra clave: keyword) and secreto alone is too: only the
# two-word labels count.
clave	(?<![^\W\d_])clave[ _-](?:de[ _-]acceso|secreta|(?:de[ _-](?:la[ _-])?)?api)	clave de acceso, Clave secreta, clave de la API, clave API, clave_secreta
credenciales	(?<![^\W\d_])credenciales	credenciales, Credenciales, db_credenciales
