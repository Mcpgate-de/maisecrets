Each scanner at its pinned version (`bench/adapters/requirements/`). The tables hold counts, never a value. The other scanners run on a fixed sample (every 50th case by the sha256 of its id, the same cases for every scanner); maisecrets runs on that sample and in full.

How to read it: the four PII datasets, the bank and the catalogs are development data (rules were written from them). **Privy is the independent number**: nobody read it for tuning, and its mapping was fixed before the first run. On Privy, maisecrets 0.6.41 has a precision of 0.44 on personal data (3,342 false positives in 28,843 cases) against 0.90 to 0.99 on the development data; Presidio, with its NER model, finds more names and ids there.
